## Economic assessment at the stated capital limits

The AUD 30 protected reserve must not fund data or losses.  The total AUD 20
short-term experimental allocation is not a data budget and cannot justify a
recurring data service by itself.  A charge of any non-trivial amount may exceed
or overwhelm that allocation before brokerage, spread, taxes and market impact.
This is a logical comparison, not a claim about any vendor's price.

Separate decisions are required:

1. **Investigation:** free public documentation/sample inspection.  The only
   presently identified no-purchase path is the Cboe PITCH sample, subject to
   its terms.
2. **Historical research:** price and licence a narrowly scoped, time-limited,
   multi-venue Tier-A pilot only after it passes sample acceptance.  Current
   lowest credible commercial cost: **unknown / QUOTE REQUIRED**.
3. **Ongoing live data:** do not subscribe merely because historical analysis is
   possible.  It would require a separately approved operating and entitlement
   budget.
4. **Trading capital:** not evaluated or approved by MICRO-002.  Data access
   cannot demonstrate that a strategy is executable or profitable.

## Pricing/rights questions that must be in a quote

Ask for all one-time extraction fees, recurring fees, exchange pass-throughs,
professional/non-professional classification, user versus device/non-display
fees, API/download fees, historical backfill charges, storage/retention caps,
derived-data rights, redistribution prohibition, audit/reporting duties and tax.
The cheapest option is unsuitable if it cannot legally retain raw data or verify
the required fields/time ordering.
