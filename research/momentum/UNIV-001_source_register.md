otherwise. "Confirmed" means the quoted text was read directly from the
cited page/document by this investigation. Where a page could not be read

explicitly rather than inferred from a garbled summary. No vendor was
contacted as part of this investigation.

## Access-date and currency caveats

- All prices quoted above are as published on the vendor's page on the
  access date stated; "provider data can change" applies to pricing pages
  exactly as it applies to market data, per this project's existing
  provenance standard (`src/download_data.py`, `data/provenance.json`).
- Siblis Research's "$378" / "$576" figures do not state a currency; they
  are reported as-is, not converted or assumed to be AUD or USD.
- U7 (S&P DJI brochure) and several institutional vendor pages (U13)
  provide confirmation that a product exists without confirming its price
  or exact contents — these are recorded as **QUOTE REQUIRED**, consistent
  with this project's standing rule never to estimate or fabricate a
  price that was not actually published.
